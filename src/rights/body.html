Home / Tools / Data rights

Ask a company to hand over, correct or delete your data.

Write a data rights request, send it from your own email account, and track the response deadline. Nothing is uploaded.

This is not legal advice

It is a free letter and deadline tool. Only jurisdictions marked Verified have a source and quote checked against the official text. Draft rules are clearly labelled and may be wrong. You decide whether a law applies to you.

Private by design. This page has no account and makes no network requests. Your letter and tracking records stay in this tab's memory only. Closing or reloading the page deletes them. Need long-term encrypted storage? Use the SecureInChat Opt-Out browser extension.

Three steps

  1. Step 1

    Choose the company

    Use the privacy address from the company's privacy policy. A company directory is planned but not built yet, so type the details yourself.

  2. Step 2

    Choose the right and the rules

    Draft, rules not verified. This jurisdiction was not checked against the official text. The letter will say so at the top. Do not treat its deadline as a legal deadline.

    Who is this request for?

    The letter is still sent from your own email address. Do not use this tool to obtain another person's data without their permission.

  3. Step 3

    Share the minimum

    Tick only what the company needs to find you and reply. Leave a box unticked and its value is never added to the letter. Never send a passport, full address or password.

    If the company asks for ID, you can cover the parts it does not need. Ask why it needs each item. You do not have to give more than is necessary to identify you.
