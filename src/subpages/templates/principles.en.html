Know what stays here—and what leaves.

This page lists, tool by tool, what is processed in your browser, which outside services a check contacts, and what each result cannot prove.

Our baseline

No accounts or analytics scripts on tool pages. Where a tool needs a network request, we name the recipient and describe its purpose.

On this page

  • Rules we follow
  • Separate data paths
  • Status labels
  • Tool by tool
  • What we cannot hide

Rules we follow

  • Files, photos and passwords are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by tools that declare local processing.
  • Tool pages do not use cookies, user accounts, advertising scripts or analytics scripts.
  • Test results are not stored on our side as personal reports. A tool may keep transient values in memory while it runs; the individual page explains its behavior.
  • Every outside service a tool contacts is listed below. The page security policy blocks other destinations.

Two different data paths

The messaging relay and tool lookup services are separate systems. The illustration below shows the difference; it is not one continuous request from the device to every service.

Messaging relay

The client design encrypts content on sender devices, sends ciphertext through the relay, and decrypts on recipient devices. This describes the intended architecture, not an independent security audit or certification.

Tool lookups

Some connection tests need an outside service to observe a public-facing value. The receiver may see the request IP address. The tool page should say which service is contacted and what is sent.

Status labels

Tested
We ran the tool ourselves and show when and how it was tested. The page runs on your device and is covered by the stated automated tests.
Documented
The information comes from project or vendor documentation linked on the page; we have not independently tested that claim.
Untested
Planned or not checked yet. Do not rely on it as a verified behavior.
Limitations
The tool works within a stated scope, but has known limits or uses an outside service. Those limits should be visible near the result.
Affiliate
A link may earn a commission. Commission does not change a test verdict or the order of results.

What we cannot hide

  • The site host and CDN, including Cloudflare, receive the network metadata required to serve a page, including the request IP address.
  • Outside services contacted by a tool can see the request information available to them.
  • Results depend on your browser and network. A passed test is not proof of anonymity or complete protection.

To verify a tool yourself, open your browser's network panel and compare the requests against the list above.
