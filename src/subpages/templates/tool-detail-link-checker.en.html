How to read the result

  1. 1

    Check the actual host

    Read the domain carefully. Text before an @ symbol or a familiar brand word may hide a different destination.

  2. 2

    Review removed parameters

    Known tracking parameters may be removable, but the cleaned link can occasionally behave differently.

  3. 3

    Use a trusted route for sign-in

    For logins or payments, open the service from a bookmark or official app instead of following a message link.

  4. 4

    Remember the limit

    No warnings does not prove that a page is safe. This tool does not inspect page content or query a phishing database.

What it checks—and what it cannot know

It can inspect tracking parameters, misleading @ patterns, raw IP addresses, international characters that resemble familiar names, plain HTTP, unusual ports, shortened URLs, brand-name clues and long subdomain chains. It cannot guarantee the destination is legitimate.
