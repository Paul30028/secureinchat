Useful privacy tools, with the limits left in.

SecureInChat aims to make everyday privacy questions easier to check. We explain what each tool does, what it sends, and what a result cannot tell you.

Why we build this

Privacy settings and technical terms can be difficult to compare. We build small tools and practical guides that help people ask focused questions, inspect results, and decide what to change next.

How we work

We prefer processing on the device when it fits the task. When a check needs an outside service, we name the service and explain what it receives. We distinguish tested behavior from vendor documentation, untested work and known limitations.

Read the privacy principles for the tool-by-tool list of data handling and external requests.

What we cannot promise

No browser test can prove complete anonymity or prevent every kind of tracking. A local checklist can help you review settings but cannot inspect whether a system setting is enabled. We do not claim an independent audit or certification unless one has actually taken place.

Corrections and contact

If a step is out of date or a source no longer supports a claim, tell us through the contact page . We aim to correct factual problems rather than hide known limits.
