## Reusable application text
SecureInChat runs free in-browser privacy tools: IP, DNS, WebRTC and IPv6 leak tests, a browser fingerprint test, a photo metadata remover and a password checker. We would place a clearly labelled partner link next to the leak tests, where visitors are checking whether their VPN works, and a password-manager link after the password checker's result. Technical articles would carry disclosed affiliate links. Test results are computed in the browser and are never changed for a partner. Traffic comes from organic search and privacy and open-source communities; we do not use paid ads, incentivised traffic or fake reviews. The site is new and traffic is still small.

## Rules we keep
- Every placement is labelled as sponsored or affiliate.
- No traffic figure we cannot show. No fake reviews. No change to results for a partner.
- Any click counting is aggregate, cookie-free and described in the privacy policy before it starts.
