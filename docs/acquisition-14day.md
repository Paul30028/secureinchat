# 14-day acquisition plan (customers first)

Source: Claude and ChatGPT discussion on 2026-10-20. Timings and automation shares are planning estimates, not statistics. No keyword volumes are claimed.

## Facts
New domain (~3 months), Google had indexed 1 page, 12 articles, 7 tools, no audience. Submitting sitemaps does not guarantee indexing and repeated re-requests do not speed it up, so stop re-requesting the same URLs.

## Channels, in order of expected speed to a real signal
1. Personalised outreach with a free custom check (days 2-7). AI: list, checks, drafts. Paul: verify list, send, handle replies.
2. Targeted community answers (days 1-7). Written or reviewed by Paul, posted by Paul, only where rules allow.
3. One prepared launch (Hacker News Show HN, or similar). Paul writes and posts the text himself; only if the demo is ready and rules fit.
4. Tool and product directories (days 3-21). Max 2 high-quality ones, submitted by Paul.
5. SEO: fix discovery first, then expand from real query data. No more mass articles.
6. Partnerships with VPN/privacy reviewers (weeks 1-6). Short personalised note from Paul.
7. Shareable results (copy/download, no server-side storage by default). Branding is optional and removable.
8. LinkedIn, manual and rule-abiding.

## Technical actions done by AI
- Home page now links to all 7 tools with plain HTML links (it linked to 1).
- Keep: 200 status, no noindex, canonical, sitemap, robots. Verified locally.
- Next: per-tool page review (what the page answers, real limits, examples). Do not resubmit URLs repeatedly.

## Schedule (Paul max 15 min per day)
D1 baseline: indexed pages, per-tool index status, clicks, real visitors. D2 15 public agency targets; Paul spot-checks. D3 custom checks on their portfolio sites; Paul verifies 3-5 reports. D4 first 3-5 emails sent by Paul. D5 improve the batch check page. D6 find 5 relevant community questions; Paul answers 1. D7 review. D8 second outreach batch. D9 photo metadata page review. D10 up to 2 directories. D11 reviewer/partner list; Paul contacts 1-2. D12 prepare launch materials. D13 launch or community answer. D14 decide which 1-2 channels to keep.

## Metrics per channel, daily
Effective visits (excluding bots, own tests, reloads), source, successful tool use (completed a test or report), real interactions, positive replies, referrals attributable to one source, index status, minutes of Paul's time.

## Ways to fool ourselves
Own tests, crawlers and link previews, scanners, bot traffic, vanity page views, praise without a concrete workflow. Success is one channel that repeatedly produces real interaction, not a nice traffic curve.
