# 7-day two-track experiment (replaces "free check to Shopify agencies")

Evidence 2026-10-20: 3 Shopify agency home pages and 15 of their 51 portfolio client sites showed no real defects; the basics (HTTPS, redirects, sitemap) are handled by the platform. One 2.1 s response was a single measurement from one location and is not evidence. Conclusion limited to: a free basic home-page check is not a convincing opener for Shopify agencies. It does not show that agencies have no need, or that other stacks behave the same. Sample bias is possible (agencies show their best work).

## Track A: question-first interviews with small WordPress maintenance teams
- Shortlist 10 small companies with public WordPress maintenance, multi-site management or hosting services; verify scope, size and a public business contact route; score with docs/outreach-playbook.md.
- Send a personalised question email, no report attached. Paul sends.
- Opener: "I'm researching how small WordPress maintenance teams monitor client sites between scheduled updates. I'm exploring a lightweight monitoring workflow and would value your practical perspective. No audit or sales pitch attached."
- Three contact layers: owner (is it worth adopting), technical lead (accuracy, false positives, workflow fit), end-client site owner (approves changes). Only test on a site the owner authorises.
- Success: 2 of 10 describe a concrete workflow or pain, and 1 is willing to test on an authorised site. Failure: no concrete feedback.

## Track B: real use of existing privacy tools
- Pick photo metadata remover and text redactor only. Check each completes its core task correctly; one user problem and page per tool.
- Take part in a few relevant communities after reading rules; help first, link only where allowed. Paul posts.
- Record real sources, completed core actions and feedback; exclude bots, own tests, reloads.
- Check index status of both pages; fix crawl, content or canonical issues. Do not resubmit URLs repeatedly.
- Success: at least 5 confirmed external uses with a known source or trigger. Failure: none, or all traffic is internal/bot.

## Decisions on day 7
A succeeds: small second interview batch about the monitoring workflow they described. B has real use: improve the best tool page and watch for repeat use. Neither: do not send more email or build an API; check target group, page exposure and traffic sources. Both: put effort into what repeatedly yields real use; keep interviews as demand discovery.
