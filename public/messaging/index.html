Cryptographic building blocks

These are design descriptions, not an independent audit or a legal certification.

ECDSA P-256

Signatures for device sign-in to the relay and admin announcements. The group key itself travels inside the invite code.

AES-256-GCM

Authenticated encryption intended to protect confidentiality and integrity of message payloads.

HKDF

Context-separated derivation of session and message keys.
