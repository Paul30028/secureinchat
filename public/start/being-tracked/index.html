Someone may be tracking me

If you think a person is following what you do, through your phone, your accounts or photos you share, these are the first checks we can offer today.

On this page

  • Do this first
  • The layers that matter here

If you are in danger, contact local emergency services or a local support organisation first. If someone has access to your phone or accounts, changing settings or passwords can alert them; consider making changes from a device they cannot reach.

Do this first

  1. Remove location data from photos before you share them Tool · runs on your device
  2. Check your passwords against known breaches without sending them Tool · runs on your device
  3. See what your browser gives away to sites that track you Tool · runs on your device
  4. What data brokers know about you, and how to ask them to delete it Guide

The layers that matter here

  • Devices and network: What your phone, computer and browser give away.
  • Accounts and email: Who can get into your accounts, and what your inbox reveals.
  • Physical: What people and devices near you can see and pick up.

We only list steps we can offer today. More guides are being written and tested; see all six layers.
