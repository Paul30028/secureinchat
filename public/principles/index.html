Privacy principles for the tools

This page lists, tool by tool, what runs on your device, what leaves it and who receives it. It complements the privacy policy. Where a limit exists we say so.

Last updated: 2026-10-21

On this page

  • Rules we follow
  • Status labels
  • Recommendations, sponsors and products
  • Tool by tool
  • What we cannot hide
  • Checking it yourself

Rules we follow

  • Files, photos and passwords are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.
  • No cookies, no accounts and no advertising or analytics scripts on the tools pages.
  • Test results are not stored on our side. The DNS test keeps resolver addresses in memory for ten minutes, plus a daily count of tests with no addresses in it.
  • Every outside service a tool contacts is listed below. A page security policy blocks any other.

Status labels

  • Tested We ran it ourselves and the page says when and how. For tools: it runs on your device and is covered by our automated tests.
  • Documented Taken from the project's or vendor's own documentation, linked on the page. We have not tested it ourselves.
  • Untested Planned, or not checked yet. Do not rely on it.
  • Limitations Works, with known limits or outside services involved. The limits are listed on the page.
  • Affiliate We may earn a commission. It never changes a verdict.

Recommendations, sponsors and products

  • We only recommend products that collect no more data than they need. Commission never changes a verdict, and every affiliate or sponsored link is labelled.
  • Any physical product we sell will be one whose protection you can check yourself. We do not sell closed-firmware security electronics under our own brand.
  • If we add an AI assistant for setup help or support, this page will say which service processes the conversation and how long it is kept before it goes live. It will never ask for passwords, recovery codes or keys.

What we cannot hide

  • Our CDN, Cloudflare, sees the IP address of every request to the site, as any web host does.
  • Outside services see your IP address when you use a tool that contacts them.
  • Results depend on your browser and network. A pass is not proof of anonymity.

Checking it yourself

The tools are plain scripts without a build step on the page. Open your browser's network panel while using one and compare the requests with the table.
