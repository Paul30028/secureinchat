Our mission

Protect your privacy layer by layer, from the device in your hand to the messages you send, with tools that run on your own device and guides where every step was actually tested.

Last updated: 2026-10-21

On this page

  • What we believe
  • Our vision
  • Who we build for
  • What we will never do
  • How we keep the lights on
  • Hold us to it

What we believe

  • Privacy is a basic right, not a premium feature. What you say, where you go and who you talk to belong to you.
  • Protection works in layers. A strong password does not help if your camera is uncovered, and an encrypted app does not help if your phone shares your location. We cover the whole stack: physical, device, accounts, communication, offline and AI.
  • Trust should be checkable. Tools run in your browser where possible, every tool lists what it sends, and every guide says what was tested and what was not.
  • Privacy must work for ordinary people. One clear action at a time, explained without jargon or fear.

Our vision

A world where anyone can live and communicate privately: free from tracking by advertisers and data brokers, from scammers and stalkers, and from surveillance by any government that acts without lawful process, and able to keep talking securely even when the network goes down.

Who we build for

Everyone who wants more control over their personal information, and especially people for whom privacy is a matter of safety: journalists, human rights workers, people leaving abusive relationships and anyone whose threat model includes more than advertisers.

What we will never do

  • Sell, share or rent data about our visitors or customers, or track them across sites.
  • Recommend a product that collects more data than it needs, however much it pays.
  • Let a sponsor or affiliate change a test result or a verdict. Every paid or affiliate placement is labelled.
  • Claim something we have not tested. Untested steps are marked as untested.
  • Sell security hardware whose firmware you cannot verify under our own brand.
  • Help anyone commit a crime or harm another person. Our guides are about protecting yourself.

How we keep the lights on

We publish this so you can judge our incentives.

  • Today: nothing is sold yet. The tools and articles are free.
  • Planned: labelled sponsors and affiliate partners that meet our privacy rules; paid tested guides; simple physical privacy products (camera covers, privacy screens, signal-blocking bags) that need no trust in firmware; and guided setup help. Each will be listed on the principles page before it starts.

Hold us to it

If anything on this site falls short of this page, email us at x@secureinchat.com. We will fix it or explain why in public.
