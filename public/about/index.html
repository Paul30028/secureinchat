About SecureInChat

Last updated: 2026-10-21

SecureInChat helps people protect their personal privacy layer by layer: physical, devices, accounts, communication, offline and AI. It started as an end-to-end encrypted messenger built on a blind relay, where keys stay on your devices and the relay only forwards ciphertext; the messenger clients are still in development. Read our mission, vision and commitments.

What you can use today

  • Relay diagnostics on the messaging page: a real WebSocket test against the production relay, with measured latency.
  • Privacy tools in /tools/, starting with the IP leak test. They run in your browser and store nothing.
  • The blog, with notes on end-to-end encryption and zero-knowledge design.

How we write about security

  • We explain why and how to verify, not only conclusions.
  • We say what a test cannot see. Our DNS check, for example, compares with an earlier run to judge a leak, and the page says when it cannot tell.
  • We separate design goals from independent audits. SecureInChat has not been through a third-party audit, and we do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001 or similar certifications.

Who runs this
