    What leaves your browser

    The connection checks are the same as the IP leak test and contact the same services: this site's /tools/api/whoami, api4.ipify.org and api6.ipify.org, ipwho.is, random names under lk.secureinchat.com with dnsapi.secureinchat.com, and stun.cloudflare.com. The IP leak test page explains what each one sees and keeps.

    The browser checks are the same as the browser fingerprint test and send nothing. The page sets no cookies, loads no analytics and serves its fonts from this site. Updated October 21, 2026.

    What the report cannot tell you

    It cannot say how rare your browser is, because that needs data about other people's browsers, which we do not collect. It does not check your phone's apps, your accounts or your router. Run it again after you change your VPN, browser or settings.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does the report check?

    Six connection checks (public IP, WebRTC, IPv6, DNS, time zone and request headers) and six browser checks (canvas, graphics card, Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track, cookies and the device details every page can read).

    Is the report saved anywhere?

    No. It is built in this tab and disappears when you close it. Copy it if you want to keep it. The page sets no cookies and loads no analytics.

    Which servers does it contact?

    The same ones as the IP leak test: this site, ipify, ipwho.is, our DNS probe and the Cloudflare STUN server. The browser checks send nothing.

    Why is something marked to fix when I am not using a VPN?

    With the VPN box unticked, connection checks only show what sites see and are not judged. Browser checks are judged the same way either way.
