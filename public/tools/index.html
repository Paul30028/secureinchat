Privacy tools

Checks that run in your browser. They store nothing and need no signup.

  • IP leak testSee your public IP, WebRTC, IPv6, time zone and header leaks while a VPN is on.
  • WebRTC leak testCheck whether a web page can read an address that goes around your VPN or proxy.
  • IPv6 leak testCheck whether your IPv6 traffic leaves outside your VPN tunnel.
  • Browser privacy reportOne run checks your IP, WebRTC, IPv6 and DNS leaks and what your browser gives away, then lists what to look at first.
  • Link checkerRemove tracking from a link and see the warning signs of phishing before you click. The link is never opened or sent.
  • Photo metadata removerSee the location, device and dates hidden in a photo and save a clean copy.
  • Browser fingerprint testSee what your browser exposes to trackers and whether your defences work.
  • Password checker and generatorEstimate strength, check known breaches without sending the password, and generate a new one.
  • Text redactorReplace emails, IP addresses, phone numbers and keys in text with placeholders before you share it.
  • Website health checkCheck a public site for HTTPS, redirects, security headers, search basics, robots.txt and a sitemap, then copy the report.
