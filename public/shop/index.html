What every product page shows

SectionWhat it contains
What it protects againstThe specific risk, in one or two sentences. No "100% secure" or "unbreakable" claims.
Who it is forWho benefits, and who does not need it.
How to check it yourselfSteps a buyer can follow without trusting us, for example a test with a phone or a multimeter.
Our test evidenceWhat we tested, on which date and how. If we have not tested it, the page says Untested.
Known limitationsWhat it does not do, and when it can fail.
Price and termsFull price, currency, one-time or recurring, and the refund rule, before the buy link.
What data is handledWhat the product itself processes, what the purchase needs (payment, email, address) and who handles it, and how long each is kept.
Supplier, delivery and returnsWho makes and delivers it, where it is available, and how returns work.
Do we earn a commission?Yes or no, and from whom. Shown next to the buy link as well.

Rules

  • A product without a test date and method is labelled Untested. One that relies on the maker's claims is labelled Documented and links to them.
  • If we earn a commission, the Affiliate label sits next to the buy link, not only at the bottom of the page.
  • The shop pages set no cookies and add no tracking. Checkout, when there is one, is run by a third party and described in the privacy policy before it goes live.

What we do not sell

CategoryWhy
Closed-firmware security electronics under our own brandYou could not check what they do, so we would be asking for blind trust. See the principles page.
Products whose business depends on tracking their usersThey conflict with the reason this site exists. See the sponsorship rules.
Anything that claims to make you anonymous or "100% secure"No product can promise that, and we do not use those words.

The full rules are on the principles and sponsorship and affiliate disclosure pages.
