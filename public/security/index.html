Report a security problem

If you find a way that a SecureInChat tool, page or service could expose someone's data, please tell us. We would rather hear it from you than from an attacker.

Last updated: 2026-10-23

On this page

  • How to report
  • What is in scope
  • Good-faith research
  • What happens next

How to report

  • Email x@secureinchat.com with a short description, the page or service, and the steps that show the problem.
  • Do not include other people's personal data. Test with your own accounts, devices and files.
  • If the problem is serious, please do not publish it until we have had a reasonable chance to fix it.

The same contact details are in our security.txt file.

What is in scope

  • This website and its tools under /tools/.
  • The tool lookup services we run: /tools/api/, dnsapi.secureinchat.com and the lk.secureinchat.com DNS test.
  • The messaging relay at wss://ws.secureinchat.com.
  • The Permission Checkup app for Android.

Outside services our tools contact, such as ipify, ipwho.is and Pwned Passwords, are run by others. Please report problems with them to their operators.

Good-faith research

We will not take action against research that follows these rules:

  • No denial-of-service testing and no automated scanning that loads the services heavily.
  • No access to, change of, or deletion of anyone else's data.
  • No social engineering of people and no physical attacks.
  • Stop and tell us as soon as you reach data that is not yours.

What happens next

We read every report and reply by email. When a report leads to a fix, we can credit you on this page if you want, under the name you choose.

We do not run a paid bug bounty at present.
