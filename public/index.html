    03 / Principles

    Privacy is a boundary you can inspect.

    Three rules shape every tool on this site. The long version and the data-flow notes are in the principles.

    1. 01

      Process locally first

      The check runs in your browser. The live text review makes no request while you type.

    2. 02

      State the limits

      The rules match formats. They do not understand names, addresses, context or unusual formats, and the result says so.

    3. 03

      You decide what happens next

      Nothing is copied, replaced, downloaded or sent until you press the button that does it.
