Sponsorship and partnerships

Last updated: 2026-10-21

SecureInChat helps people protect their privacy layer by layer, from physical gear to accounts, messaging and AI use. We accept a small number of sponsors and affiliate partners whose products respect the same principle. See our mission.

Who can sponsor

  • Products that collect no more data than they need to work, explain their data handling clearly and do not sell user data.
  • Examples of categories we cover: encrypted email, password managers, security keys, privacy-respecting browsers and software, and physical privacy gear.
  • We decline products whose business depends on tracking users, and we may decline any product we cannot test or describe accurately.

What we offer

  • A labelled sponsor placement on the tools pages, at most one per page.
  • A mention in a blog post that explains a technical topic, marked as sponsored.
  • Affiliate links in articles, with a visible disclosure next to each link.

Our rules

  • Test results are computed in the visitor's browser. A sponsor cannot change them or the wording of a verdict.
  • Every paid or affiliate placement is labelled as such.
  • We only list products we can describe accurately. We may ask to test a product before we mention it.
  • We do not add trackers for sponsors. Any measurement is aggregate, such as clicks, and is described in the privacy policy before it starts.

Traffic

The site is new. We will share real figures on request once there are enough to be meaningful, and we will not quote numbers we cannot show.
