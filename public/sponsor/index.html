How SecureInChat is funded

Last updated: 2026-10-21

SecureInChat is free to use and does not track you. If money ever changes hands, this page says how. See our mission.

What sponsors can and cannot do

Sponsors may support the project or a clearly labelled piece of content. Sponsors:

  • cannot buy a ranking, a "best" or "safest" label, or a test result;
  • do not review, edit or approve content before it is published;
  • cannot ask us to add any script, pixel, cookie or tracking link;
  • receive no visitor data. At most, they get our aggregate page-view counts.

Who we do not accept

We do not accept money from ad networks, data brokers, surveillance vendors, or companies whose main business is behavioural tracking. We can end a sponsorship at any time if it conflicts with these rules. Past sponsorships stay listed on this page.

Commission links

Some English tool pages carry commission links to NordVPN and NordPass (Nord Security affiliate programme). Next to them we say that we may earn a commission, link to the same sites without a commission, and state that we have not yet tested the products ourselves. A commission never changes a test result.
