Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

This policy covers secureinchat.com, including the tools under /tools/. It describes what the site does with your data today. If that changes, for example if we add advertising, we will update this page before the change goes live.

What we do not do

  • We do not set cookies and we do not use local storage on this site.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or give us your name or email address.
  • We do not store the results of any tool you run.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and request details when your browser loads a page, and processes them to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Aggregate analytics

The main pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures page views and performance without cookies and without tracking you across sites. The tools pages under /tools/ load no analytics.

Relay diagnostics

The home page opens a WebSocket connection from your browser to our relay at ws.secureinchat.com to measure latency and verify the relay's challenge. No message content is involved. The relay and Cloudflare see the connection the way any server sees a visitor, including your IP address.

The IP leak test

The test runs in your browser. When you press the button, your browser contacts these services, and each of them necessarily sees your IP address:

  • This site (/tools/api/whoami) returns the address, network and approximate location Cloudflare saw, plus a few request headers. It writes nothing to disk.
  • api4.ipify.org and api6.ipify.org return your IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.
  • ipwho.is looks up the network of a second address, only when the test finds one.
  • stun.cloudflare.com answers the WebRTC check, as it does for video calls.

Those requests are sent without cookies and without a referrer.

Advertising and affiliate links

The site does not currently show advertising. If we add ads or affiliate links, they will be labeled, this page will be updated first, and third-party ad providers may then use cookies under their own policies.

Your choices and contact

Because we do not keep personal data about visitors, there is little to access or delete. For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub. Our contact details are on the contact page.
