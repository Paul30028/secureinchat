Privacy policy

This policy covers secureinchat.com, including the tools under /tools/. It describes what the site does with your data today. If that changes, for example if we add advertising, we will update this page before the change goes live.

Last updated: 2026-10-21

On this page

  • What we do not do
  • Hosting and server logs
  • Aggregate analytics
  • Relay diagnostics
  • The IP leak test
  • Advertising and affiliate links

A tool-by-tool list is on the privacy principles page.

What we do not do

  • We do not set cookies and we do not use local storage on this site.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or give us your name or email address.
  • We do not store the results of any tool you run.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and request details when your browser loads a page, and processes them to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Aggregate analytics

The main pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures page views and performance without cookies and without tracking you across sites. The tools pages under /tools/ load no analytics.

Relay diagnostics

The messaging page opens a WebSocket connection from your browser to our relay at ws.secureinchat.com when it loads to measure latency and verify the relay's challenge. No message content is involved. The relay and Cloudflare see the connection the way any server sees a visitor, including your IP address.

The IP leak test

The test runs in your browser. When you press the button, your browser contacts these services, and each of them necessarily sees your IP address:

  • This site (/tools/api/whoami) returns the address, network and approximate location Cloudflare saw, plus a few request headers. It writes nothing to disk.
  • api4.ipify.org and api6.ipify.org return your IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.
  • ipwho.is looks up the network of a second address when the test finds one, and the network of each DNS resolver.
  • Random names under lk.secureinchat.com are looked up by your system for the DNS check. The DNS server we run for that domain records the address of the resolver that asks, keeps it in memory for ten minutes and writes no log. It also keeps one count per day of how many tests reached it and how many results were read back; this is a number only and holds no address. To stop one source from inflating that count, a per-resolver counter is held in memory for up to an hour and is never written out. That address can be your internet provider's resolver, a public resolver or, if your system resolves names itself, your own address. The server's hosting provider can see network traffic to it, as any host can.
  • dnsapi.secureinchat.com returns the resolver addresses recorded for your run, and only yours. It sees your IP address when your browser asks.
  • api.pwnedpasswords.com answers the password checker's breach check, only when you press its button. Your browser sends the first five characters of the password's SHA-1 hash, never the password or the full hash. The service sees your IP address and that prefix.
  • stun.cloudflare.com answers the WebRTC check, as it does for video calls.

Those requests are sent without cookies and without a referrer.

Advertising and affiliate links

The site does not show advertising. Some pages carry commission links to Nord Security products (NordVPN, NordPass, NordLocker, Coveron), each marked as an affiliate link. The pages themselves load nothing from Nord Security. If you click one, your browser goes through Nord Security's tracking domain, which records the click and may set cookies under its own privacy policy. Each link has a plain alternative without a commission.

Your choices and contact

Because we do not keep personal data about visitors, there is little to access or delete. For privacy questions, email x@secureinchat.com.
