A one-minute checklist before you paste anything into an AI assistant

A short checklist of what to remove before pasting text, documents or photos into an AI assistant, with the free browser tools that help and the things no tool can find for you.

By Paul2026-10-20

This list is about what you hand over, not about any provider's promises, which differ and change. Spend a minute on it before you paste or upload.

The checklist

  1. Do you need to share the original at all? Often a few lines or a described pattern is enough.
  2. Secrets: API keys, tokens, passwords in connection strings, private keys. Rotate any secret that has already been pasted somewhere.
  3. Contact data: emails, phone numbers, addresses. Replace them with placeholders.
  4. Financial data: card numbers, IBANs, account numbers, statements.
  5. Other people's data: names, messages and details of people who did not agree to be shared.
  6. Photos: hidden location, device and time data, and anything visible in the picture that gives away where you are.
  7. Work material: check your employer's rules before pasting internal or client documents.

Tools that help (they run in your browser and upload nothing)

  • Text redactor: emails, phone numbers, IP and MAC addresses, card numbers, IBANs, common API key formats and private key blocks.
  • Photo metadata remover: shows and removes EXIF data in JPEG, PNG and WebP photos.

What no tool can find for you

Names, street addresses, plain account numbers, employer names and anything that only makes sense in context. Read the text again before you send it. More detail: bank statements, CVs, photos.
