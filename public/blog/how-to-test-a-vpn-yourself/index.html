How to test a VPN yourself without trusting the vendor

A short checklist for checking what a VPN does and does not hide: public IP, WebRTC, IPv6, DNS, a dropped connection, and what no test can tell you.

By Paul2026-10-20

A VPN vendor's page says what the product is meant to do. A few free checks show what it does on your device, on your network, today. None of them proves a provider's logging claims, and the last section says what they cannot cover.

1. Compare addresses with and without the VPN

Run the IP leak test with the VPN off and note the address and network. Turn the VPN on and run it again. The address and network should change. If they do not, the VPN is not carrying that traffic.

2. Check the side routes

The WebRTC and IPv6 tests look for addresses that go around the tunnel. These are the two most common failures a browser can detect.

3. Check DNS

A browser cannot see which DNS resolver answers it, so a DNS test needs its own DNS server. Our IP leak test has one. Run it with the VPN off, then on, and compare the resolvers. A resolver run by your internet provider while the VPN is on is a leak. If you use a public resolver such as Google or Cloudflare, you will see that one in both runs, and the test cannot say who chose it.

4. Drop the connection

With a page loaded, turn off the network adapter for a few seconds or disconnect the VPN manually, then see whether traffic stops. A working kill switch blocks traffic when the tunnel is down. Run the tests again after you reconnect.

5. Try a second network

Repeat on mobile data or a different Wi-Fi. Leaks often depend on the network, particularly IPv6 and UDP handling.

What no test here can show

  • Whether the provider keeps logs. That depends on its servers, its policies and independent audits, not on anything a browser can measure.
  • Whether your traffic is safe once it leaves the VPN. A VPN moves where you appear to connect from. It does not make a website you log into forget who you are.
  • Behaviour in other apps. A browser test covers the browser. A torrent client or a game can take a different route.

Treat the results as a snapshot. Re-run the checks after you update the VPN client, the browser or the operating system.
